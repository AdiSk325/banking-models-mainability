## Purpose

This procedure provides step-by-step instructions for requesting, classifying, approving, and implementing model changes.

---

## Steps to Be Documented

<!-- TODO: Write detailed step-by-step procedure aligned with STD-005. -->

### Step 1 — Identify and Document the Change
- Who may initiate a change request.
- Complete [TMPL-004 — Change Request Form](../03_templates/TMPL-004_change_request_form.md).
- Required information: change description, reason, impact assessment, proposed timeline.

### Step 2 — Classify the Change
- Use materiality criteria from [STD-005](../01_supporting_standards/STD-005_change_management_standard.md).
- Submit to MRM for classification confirmation.
- Handle disputes: who resolves, how.

### Step 3A — Non-Material Change Process
- Documentation update requirements.
- Model Owner sign-off.
- Inventory update.
- Timeline expectations.

### Step 3B — Material Change Process
- Scope of re-development required.
- Re-testing requirements.
- Revalidation scope (full or partial).
- Re-approval governance path (by tier).
- Re-deployment controls.
- Timeline expectations.

### Step 4 — Emergency Change Process
- Definition of emergency.
- Who may invoke emergency protocol.
- Expedited approval pathway.
- Retrospective documentation and governance requirements.
- Escalation and reporting to MRM and governance.

### Step 5 — Post-Change
- Version log update.
- Inventory update.
- Communication to stakeholders.
- Monitoring plan update if required.
