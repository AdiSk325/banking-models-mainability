## Purpose

This procedure provides step-by-step instructions for requesting, approving, tracking, and closing exceptions to the requirements of the Model Lifecycle Guide and its supporting standards.

### Step 1 — Identify the Need for an Exception
- What constitutes a valid exception (not a workaround or a gap in process).
- Who may request an exception.
- What information must be prepared.

### Step 2 — Prepare Exception Request
- Required content:
  - Which requirement is being departed from (reference to Guide chapter / standard).
  - Reason for the exception (why compliance is not possible / practical).
  - Proposed duration.
  - Proposed compensating controls.
  - Proposed remediation plan (how the exception will be resolved).
  - Risk assessment of granting the exception.

### Step 3 — Submit and Review
- Submit to MRM for review.
- MRM assessment: risk of exception vs. cost/feasibility of compliance.
- MRM classification: minor exception (MRM function head) vs. material exception (Model Risk Committee).

### Step 4 — Approval
- Minor exception: MRM function head approval, documented.
- Material exception: escalated to Model Risk Committee.
- Documentation of decision with rationale.

### Step 5 — Implement Compensating Controls
- Confirm compensating controls are in place before model proceeds.
- Document controls and responsible party.

### Step 6 — Exception Register
- Add to MRM exception register.
- Include: model ID, requirement departed from, duration, compensating controls, remediation plan, approval reference.

### Step 7 — Monitor and Review
- MRM reviews open exceptions at defined frequency (<!-- TODO: quarterly? -->).
- Alert when exception approaches expiry.
- Confirm whether remediation is on track.

### Step 8 — Close the Exception
- Confirm remediation is complete.
- Remove from active exception register.
- Update inventory.
